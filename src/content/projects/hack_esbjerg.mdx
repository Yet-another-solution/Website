## What was Hack Esbjerg 2026?

Hack Esbjerg 2026 was a 24-hour hackathon in Esbjerg, Denmark, held on 19–20 March 2026 and organised by [Y-A-S ApS](/). It was open to all skill levels: teams turned ideas into working software in a single day and night, then presented what they had built.

## The event in numbers

- **100+** participants
- **13** projects built in 24 hours
- **6** prize categories

## What we did

[Y-A-S ApS](/) organised the event. Organising hackathons and community events is one of the two things we do, next to building custom web applications in C# and Blazor.

## What's next

The event has its own website, [hackesbjerg.dk](https://hackesbjerg.dk/), where news about future editions is published. If you are planning a hackathon or a community event of your own, get in touch.
